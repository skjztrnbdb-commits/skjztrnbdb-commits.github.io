How it is made

One person designs the games and playtests them until they break; an LLM writes the code. There are no art assets: everything on screen is geometry, text and layout; every sound effect is synthesized, and the music is a chiptune soundtrack. Phone only, portrait only, because these are games for the subway and the plane.
