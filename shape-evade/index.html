SHAPE EVADE

Survivors · iOS · in beta

A survivors game made of nothing but geometry. Circles, triangles and hexagons come at you from every side, your weapons fire on their own, and you pick one card of three every level. Five-minute runs, made for a phone held upright.

The one rule

Your power is a chain of multipliers, and the chain is always on screen: BASE × MIGHT × RATE × VOLLEY × AREA × CRIT = POWER. Every card says what it will do to that number before you take it: POWER 248 → 322. No "+5 damage", no card you regret, no stat hidden in a menu.

What is in it

  • Six weapons, each with an evolution that changes how it behaves, not how hard it hits. The recipe is written on the weapon's card.
  • Six heroes, each a different polygon, each starting with its own weapon.
  • You cannot stand still. Enemies spawn to hold a density, not a schedule: the faster you clear the screen, the faster they come.
  • Events: an elite that drops a chest, a maze whose walls hold only you, a siege circle, an eclipse, a caravan of chests crossing the field.

How it is made

One HTML file, no libraries, no sprites, no images: everything on screen is drawn from geometry. No audio files either: every sound is synthesized and the music is generated live. It collects nothing and shows no ads.

Play it
