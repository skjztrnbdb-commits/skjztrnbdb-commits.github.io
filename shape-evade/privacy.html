SHAPE EVADE

Privacy Policy

Shape Evade collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything it remembers stays on your device.

What is stored

The game saves your progress — unlocked heroes, upgrades, best results, the run you are in the middle of, and your sound, music and language settings — in local storage on the device itself. None of it leaves the phone, and none of it is readable by anyone but the app.

What is not collected

No account, no name, no email, no location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising identifier, no analytics, no crash reporting, no cookies, no third-party software development kits of any kind.

Advertising and tracking

There is no advertising in Shape Evade and no tracking across apps or websites. The app never asks for permission to track, because it has nothing to track you with.

Children

The game is safe for any age in this respect: since nothing at all is collected, nothing is collected from children either.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and everything it stored goes with it. There is also a Reset progress button on the title screen that erases saved progress without deleting the app.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting anything, this page will say so before that version ships.
