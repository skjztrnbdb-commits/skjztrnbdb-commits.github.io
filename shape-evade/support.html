SHAPE EVADE

Support

A survivors game made entirely of geometry. No sprites, no photographs, no text you have to read while something is chasing you.

Getting help

Email me@velikanov.pro. It is one person answering, so please say which device and which iOS version you are on, and what happened just before the problem.

Common questions

My progress disappeared. Progress lives on the device only. Deleting the app deletes it, and it does not travel between devices.

There is no sound. Check the speaker and note buttons in the bottom-right corner of the title screen — sound and music switch separately. Also check the silent switch on the side of the phone.

A run I was in the middle of is gone. An unfinished run is saved and offered as Continue run. Abandoning it, or finishing it, clears it.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is nothing to buy, no advertising, and no subscription.
